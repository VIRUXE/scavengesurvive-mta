Reborn vs the original

Every difference, with receipts

Scavenge & Survive Reborn is a faithful port: the same 345 item types, loot tables, maps, vehicles, recipes and rules as Southclaws' SA-MP gamemode, checked line by line against the Pawn source. This page lists everything that is not the same, and why. Each entry links to the design document, review, runbook or commit that records it.

New: no upstream equivalent Changed: upstream did it differently Dropped or deferred
Honesty note: the two-client live checks for the late milestones are still open, the camper stove is a model that does not cook yet, and the Fort Defiance rebuild stopped at the rooftop walkways. The README's milestone table is the live status.
