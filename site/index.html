The four rules

Nothing is yours until you are dead with it

Scavenge & Survive has never been about levels or money. Supplies are scarce, everyone wants the same things, and safety in numbers is the only safety there is. Trust is the rarest loot on the map.

One alive character

Your nickname is your account. When your character dies, it is gone: skills, wounds, hunger and everything on it. The next one wakes up on a beach with a bandage.

Everything is an item

No menus full of abstract inventory. A pistol, a bandage, a crate, a car door: all of it lies on the ground as a real object, can be picked up, dropped, stolen, put in a trunk or buried in a safebox.

Wounds bleed

Every hit is a wound with a calibre, a body part and a bleed rate. A dirty bandage stops it and infects you; an antiseptic one does not. Untreated, you bleed out walking home.

Death drops it all

Your body stays, your things land in a ring around it, and whoever gets there first keeps them. Combat-logging does not save you: the server finishes the fight without you.

What you will be doing

The original's five promises, plus one it never made

Southclaws' site summed the game up in five slates. All five are here, and the port adds a sixth: vehicles are now machines with batteries, starters, dashboards and salvageable bodywork.

Objective
  • The objective is still up to you.
  • Survive with a friend, a group or alone.
  • Build a stable society, or burn it down.
  • No safe zones, no staff events: the players set the rules.
Survival
  • Hunger falls all the time, faster when you run.
  • Wounds bleed until bandaged, and infect if the bandage is dirty.
  • Raw meat and fish infect you: cook them on a campfire.
  • Seven drugs stack, and every one of them has a price.
Building
  • Walls, floors, doors and keypads anywhere in the world.
  • A plot pole claims the 20 metres around it.
  • Explosives pass through stacked walls: a fort is only as strong as its skin.
  • Safeboxes and tents keep your things while you are gone.
Combat
  • Server-checked wounds with calibre, ammo type and distance falloff.
  • Magazines reload on R; hold N to unload.
  • Knockouts, handcuffs, disarm, surrender and the aim shout.
  • Firearms are deadly, if you can spare the ammo. Melee and explosives are always there.
Crafting
  • 46 recipes: combine in hand, construct on the ground, build at a Workbench.
  • Scrap machines, refining machines, water purifiers and generators.
  • Gardening, fishing and cooking.
  • Skills shorten every hold the more you use them.
Driving
New in Reborn
  • Fuel burns and spills. Batteries drain and recharge. Starters wear out.
  • A dashboard with warning lamps, a tacho and a voltmeter.
  • Twelve Bobcat parts you wrench off and fit back on.
  • A towable Airstream you can weld armour onto.

New in Reborn

Scarcity is a budget, not a timer

The original re-rolled the map every five hours. Reborn gives every zone a carrying capacity and refills only what is missing, slowly, with cooldowns, and never within 150 metres of a player.

What players carry still counts against the budget. A clan that hoards two hundred rifles in a safebox is starving its own map; the server does not notice the difference between a crate on the ground and a crate in a fort.

Vehicles work the same way: about eighty on the map at once, abandoned ones retired after three days, each new one spawning smoking, half-wrecked and almost out of fuel.

New in Reborn

Cars are machines now

The original had fuel, locks and a bonnet to repair. Reborn opens the bonnet properly: a battery that drains and recharges, a starter motor with a life of its own, a dashboard that tells you which of them just let you down, and bodywork you can take off and sell.

The dashboard

BAT, CHK, FUEL and LGT lamps, a segmented tacho and a voltmeter in the HUD's own style. Turn the key: a flat battery clicks, a wrecked engine grinds, an empty tank cranks evenly, a start does the bulb check and the needle catches.

Batteries and starters

Parked cars lose charge, lights left on drain it fast, driving puts it back. The battery comes out from under the bonnet, so does a dead starter. A Starter Motor lasts 60 to 400 starts; 2 % of cars spawn with one that is already gone.

Salvage and armour

Twelve Bobcat parts, good or bad, come off with a Wrench and go back on in an empty slot; crashes knock them loose. The Airstream hitches to anything and takes welded metal panels, 180 kg and 8 % acceleration each, until an angle grinder takes them off.

Reborn vs the original

Same game. Different engine room.

Everything the SA-MP version did is here, by the numbers and by the feel. What is different is the machinery underneath, and the things that machinery made possible. Six of the sixty-odd differences:

The original (SA-MP)
Reborn (MTA:SA)
wasItems lived in files; a crash lost up to a minute, a restart re-rolled the world.
nowEvery item is a MariaDB row with a spatial index. Every move is one atomic procedure. Restarts lose nothing.
wasHealth was whatever the client reported.
nowThe server checks the shooter's weapon, range and rate and computes the wound. A client cannot snap it back.
wasHats and masks fitted on 17 tuned skins; the hollow houses had empty window holes and oversized doors.
nowAll 310 human peds wear them, 22 helmeted skins have hatless twins, 75 windows are glazed, 571 doors fitted.
wasMap objects through a streamer plugin, SA-MP-only models and all.
now23,211 objects built on your client in about 1.3 s, the 209 SA-MP-only models shipped as custom models.
wasLanguage files in mixed encodings, about 150 texts missing per language.
nowTen languages, 554 texts each, every one filled, items included.
wasBans by name or IP; mutes ended when you relogged.
nowBans by name, IP and serial with durations, mutes that persist, every staff action logged.

Why Multi Theft Auto

The platform is the point

Scavenge & Survive was always pushing against what SA-MP could do. MTA:SA is open source, still released, scripted in Lua on both sides, and lets a server ship its own models, draw its own interface and build the world on the client.

Custom models

Load DFF, TXD and COL files from a script. That is how the Airstream, the Bobcat parts, the cut hats, the hatless twins and the enterable trailers exist.

A client that draws

dx drawing, render targets, 3D models in the UI, SVG, shaders. The SA-MP look rebuilt pixel-close, with live item previews the original could never show.

Honest sync

Limb-level hits, cancellable client damage and sync-time contexts make a server-authoritative wound model possible instead of a hope.

Alive

Open source since 2008, 1.6 in 2023, nightlies since. SA-MP's last client shipped in 2015. The functions this port leans on did not exist two years ago.
