# Vehicle Salvage Parts

![Bobcat](images/vehicles/422_bobcat.png)

Every car and truck's body comes apart. Its hood, trunk or tailgate, doors and bumpers are items: a Ratchet takes them off, a crash knocks them off, and a part in hand goes back onto a vehicle of the same kind that is missing it. Bikes, boats, aircraft, trailers and a few odd vehicles (the Rhino, the Baggage, the Tug, the Bandito, the Vortex) have none.

<!-- shot:shot-bobcat-salvage -->

## The parts

Every part comes as two items, *Good* and *Bad*, made from the vehicle's own intact and damaged panels. A Good part is intact or only dented; a Bad one hangs broken, with the glass smashed on the doors. A vehicle has the slots its body has: a sedan has a trunk and four doors, a pickup a tailgate and two doors, a truck cab only a hood, two doors and a front bumper.

Parts belong to their vehicle: a Sentinel hood fits only a Sentinel. Vehicles with the same body share one set: the Los Santos and San Fierro Police Cars, and the Rancher and the Ranger. Variants of one vehicle (the Sabre and the Scimitar, the Linerunner and the Truckfort) share theirs too.

The loot weight is per part in the industrial loot table. Each vehicle's share follows how often it spawns, so parts of common cars turn up often and parts of rare trucks seldom, the Bad ones twice as often as the Good. Parts also turn up in vehicle trunks, at about a third of that.

Every part is size 6 and is carried in both hands like a crate: it never goes into a bag or the inventory, you cannot get into a vehicle while holding one, and it rides in a trunk or a box ([Inventory](Inventory)).

## Taking a part off

1. Hold a **Ratchet** ([Ratchet](Ratchet)).
2. Stand within 1.8 m of the part. The vehicle must be unlocked, standing still, with its engine off. The key list reads `(Hold) Remove Left Door` or the part you are nearest to.
3. Hold `F` for half a second (a tap at the bonnet is the Ratchet's repair of a slightly broken engine instead). A 4 second hold follows, shorter with the Repair [skill](Skills). A part that is still on the vehicle takes the hold before the battery does, including the hood.

The part lands on the ground beside its slot: a Good item if it was whole or dented, a Bad one if it hung broken. Walking more than 0.8 m, changing what you hold, getting into a vehicle or dying cancels the hold, and only one person can work on a vehicle at a time. A slot that is already empty says there is no part left there to take off.

## Putting a part on

Hold the part, stand by its slot on a vehicle it fits and press `F` (`Install body part` in the key list). The slot must be **empty**: to replace a dented door you take the old one off first. The 4 second hold has the same cancel rules. A Good part leaves the slot as new, a Bad part leaves it dented and hanging, but on the vehicle.

A part put on by hand is only **loose**: *Body part fitted loosely, tighten it with a Ratchet or it will shake off*. Hold a Ratchet at it and hold `F` (`(Hold) Tighten Left Door`): 3 seconds, and *Body part tightened*. Tightening trains the Repair skill. A tightened part stays on until a Ratchet takes it off or a crash does.

## Loose parts shake off

Drive with a loose part on and it comes off by itself:

- above 15 km/h there is a chance every second that one loose part flies off, growing with speed until at 75 km/h it is almost certain;
- braking hard (losing 25 km/h within a second) throws off every loose part at once.

A part that shakes off is flung ahead of the vehicle, up to 12 m, and lands as the item it was, Good or Bad.

## Crash damage

When a crash tears a part off, it drops onto the ground beside its slot as a **Bad** item, so a wreck can be stripped for parts even without a Ratchet. Parts that were already missing when the vehicle spawned never drop.
