# Containers & Bags

Storage comes in two kinds: **bags**, which you wear on your back, and **containers**, boxes and crates that stand in the world. Both are measured in [slots](Item-Sizes), the same units as your 6-slot [inventory](Inventory).

## Bags

- Hold the bag and press `Y` to put it on. You can wear one bag at a time, and not while a parachute is on your back (and the other way round).
- With empty hands, press `N` to take the bag off into your hands.
- Items you put away go into your inventory first. When it is full they go into the bag. Open the bag from the extra row at the bottom of the inventory list, or from the `BACK` tile.
- The bag keeps its contents when you take it off, drop it or hand it to someone.
- A bag in your hands opens with `F`, so you can fill it before putting it on.
- A Parachute Bag is made by combining a Parachute with a Combat Knife (kept); see [Combining](Combining) and [Parachute](Parachute). The Heart Shaped Box is a 6-slot bag; see [Small Items](Small-Items).

## Containers in the world

- Tap `F` within 2 m of a container to open it. Its list appears next to your inventory, titled with its name and `(used/slots)`: its rows offer `Move to Inventory` (and `Move to <bag>`), your own rows `Move to <container>`, and with something in your hands an `<Empty>` row offers `Store in <container>`; `Y` also puts the held item straight in. `Equip` takes an item out into your hands, so empty them first. The container stays yours to work only while you are within 4 m of it. What you leave inside is saved across server restarts.
- To pick a container up, hold `F` for half a second at it: it comes into your hands with everything inside. A bag lying on the ground works the same way: a tap opens it, a hold picks it up.
- The boxes, crates, cartons and bin bags that are part of the map (in warehouses, yards and piles) are containers too, many already filled with loot. They are bulky: carried in both hands and never put in a bag or inventory ([Inventory](Inventory)). A spot that lost its box gets a new one after a while; see [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).
- The **Locker** stands as tall as you and is solid. It opens only from its door side: stand in front of it facing it, no more than 60 degrees off either way, or you are told *Open the locker from the front, facing its door*. Everyone sees its door open while someone has it open.
- The Cupboard and Gun Case are [furniture](Furniture), not boxes: each displays one item, put in and taken out with `F`. Desks and tables hold items on top. See [Furniture](Furniture).
