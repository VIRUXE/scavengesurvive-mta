# Item Sizes

Every item has a **size** between 1 and 20. The size is the number of slots the item takes up when it is stored.

## Where sizes matter

| Place | Capacity |
|:--|--:|
| Your inventory (pockets) | 6 slots |
| A worn bag | 6 to 19 slots, see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags) |
| A pitched [tent](Tent) | 16 slots |
| A box, crate or locker in the world | 2 to 16 slots, see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags) |
| A machine's container | 12 slots, see [Machines](Machines) |

A container holds items whose sizes add up to at most its capacity. A size 3 item in a 6-slot inventory leaves 3 slots. When nothing more fits you are told that extra slots are required, and the item stays where it was.

## What it means in play

- You hold one item at a time in your hands, whatever its size. `Y` puts it away into your inventory; if the inventory is full, it goes into your worn bag. See [Inventory](Inventory).
- An item bigger than 6 cannot go into your pockets. It can only be carried in your hands, or stored in a bag or container with enough free slots.
- A bag is an item too: a Backpack takes 4 slots of space when you carry it, and gives more than that when you wear it.
