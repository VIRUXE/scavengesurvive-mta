# World & Zones

The whole of San Andreas is open: three cities, the countryside between them and the desert in the north. There are no safe areas. The server divides the map into seven **zones**; they decide how much loot each region may hold (see [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn)) and name the places in chat announcements.

## The seven zones

| Zone | What it covers |
|:--|:--|
| **Los Santos** | The south-eastern city with its airport, docks, Ganton, Market, Jefferson and Las Colinas |
| **San Fierro** | The western city: Downtown, the Financial district, Chinatown, Doherty, Easter Bay Airport and the Easter Basin naval base |
| **Las Venturas** | The north-eastern city, its airport, Roca Escalante, Come-A-Lot and the K.A.C.C. Military Fuels depot |
| **Red County** | The farmland in the middle of the map: Blueberry, Dillimore, Montgomery, Palomino Creek and Fisher's Lagoon |
| **Flint County** | The south-west: the fields south of San Fierro, Whetstone, Angel Pine and Mount Chiliad |
| **Bone County** | The desert in the north centre: Fort Carson, the Hunter Quarry, El Castillo del Diablo, Verdant Meadows and the Restricted Area ([Area 69](Area-69)) |
| **Tierra Robada** | The north-west: Bayside, El Quebrados and the ranch country along the bay |

## Where the loot is

Every zone has a fixed set of loot points and may hold 2 items per point, so the cities are far richer than the countryside: Los Santos has about 4,000 loot points, San Fierro 3,200, Las Venturas 2,000, Red County 650, Bone County 550, Flint County 500 and Tierra Robada 180. How and when they fill is on [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/VIRUXE/scavengesurvive-mta/images/map/loot.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="View full-size map; Ctrl/Cmd-click or middle-click to open in a new tab"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/VIRUXE/scavengesurvive-mta/images/map/loot.png" alt="Where the loot points are: the denser, the more spawn points nearby"></a>

Each point is of one kind, and the kind decides what it can hold:

- **Civilian** (most points: homes, shops, streets): tools, food and drink, bags, clothes and hats, pistols, shotguns and the odd rifle, household odds and ends.
- **Industrial** (yards, warehouses, garages): tools, electronics and batteries, boxes, building panels, gates and doors, petrol cans, gas masks, now and then a chainsaw, welder or generator.
- **Survivor** (camps): medical items, bags, lighters and campfires, food, guns and ammunition.
- **Medical**: bandages, medkits, doctor's bags, pills and auto-injectors, at Santa Flora, Market, Jefferson, Las Venturas Airport, Fort Carson, Montgomery, El Quebrados, Angel Pine, Palomino Creek and inside the Restricted Area.
- **Police**: handcuffs, stun guns, police caps, shields, pistols, shotguns and rifles, at Pershing Square, Downtown San Fierro, Queens, Easter Bay Airport, Roca Escalante, The Emerald Isle, Dillimore, Palomino Creek, Angel Pine, Las Payasadas and Fort Carson.
- **Military**: armour and helmets, parachutes, grenades and explosives, assault rifles, sniper rifles, the rocket launcher, flamer and minigun (very rare) and heavy ammunition. It lies almost only in four places: the Restricted Area ([Area 69](Area-69)), the Easter Basin naval base, Downtown San Fierro and the K.A.C.C. Military Fuels depot.

## Weather

![Sandstorm](images/screens/sandstorm.jpg)

The weather is the same for everyone and changes on its own. A change never comes sooner than 10 minutes after the last one; from then on there is a 10 % chance every minute of a new roll, so a spell of weather lasts about 20 minutes on average, sometimes much longer. Every weather of San Andreas can come up, but rain, the sandstorm and the dull countryside skies are drawn only a third to a half as often as the clear ones, and the server never starts up in a sandstorm.

Rain matters: a campfire burns out fast in it ([Campfire & BBQ](Campfire-and-BBQ)), and it does not rain indoors ([Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers)). LSD and heroin change the sky for you alone ([Drugs](Drugs)).

## Day and night

The in-game clock follows the real clock of the server: one game minute per real minute, a full day and night every 24 hours. Nothing speeds it up.

## Where you spawn

A new character wakes up at one of 14 spots at the water's edge, from the ocean shore west of San Fierro round to the south coast of Flint County and on the inland shores of Red County, Tierra Robada and Bone County, never the same one twice in a row. The map of them and what you start with are on [Death](Death).
