# Trees & Wood

![Chainsaw](images/items/341_chnsaw.png) ![Wood Log](images/items/19793_firewoodlog1.png)

About 3,000 of the trees standing around San Andreas can be felled with a **Chainsaw** for **Wood Logs**: pines, firs and redwoods, elms, ashes, cedars, hazels, locusts and spruces, the Joshua trees of the desert and one kind of palm, in 77 sizes. Only these count (a bush or a tree of another kind cannot be cut), and only out in the open, not inside buildings.

## Felling a tree

1. Hold the Chainsaw and stand against the trunk: within the tree's own thickness plus 1 metre of its foot, and no more than 3 m above or below it.
2. Hold `F`. You turn to the tree and saw for as long as the key is down; a bar shows how long the rest of the tree takes. Letting go of `F`, stepping more than 0.8 m away, getting into a vehicle or dying stops the cut.
3. A tree keeps the cut it has taken: you or anyone else can carry on later, until the server restarts.
4. The last cut fells it. The tree tips over (or sinks into the ground) and is gone for everyone; 4 seconds later its Wood Logs lie around the stump, within 1.5 m.

Felling needs no petrol in the saw. The [Forestry skill](Skills) grows with every tree you fell and shortens the cut, down to half the time.

How long a tree takes and what it gives, with no skill:

| Trees | Time to fell | Wood Logs |
|:--|--:|--:|
| Joshua trees (desert) | 3–5 s | 2–3 |
| Elms, ashes, cedars, hazels, locusts, spruces | 3–12 s | 4–15 |
| Pines | 6–13 s | 6–16 |
| Other broadleaf trees and big bushes | 6–25 s | 8–24 |
| Palms | 16 s | 3 |
| Firs and redwoods | 6–42 s | 7–38 |

## Regrowth

A felled tree stands again 3 weeks after it fell, server restarts included. Until then there is nothing where it stood.

## Wood Logs

A Wood Log is a size 1 item. What they are for:

- A **Campfire**: 3 Wood Logs lit with a Lighter, see [Campfire & BBQ](Campfire-and-BBQ). A burnt-out campfire leaves Burnt Logs, not Wood Logs.
- A **Workbench**: 4 Wood Logs and 2 Refined Metal built with a Hammer, see [Workbench](Workbench).
- **Furniture**: a Desk (3 logs), a Table (4), a Barstool (2) or a Small Table (4) with a Screwdriver, see [Constructing](Constructing) and [Furniture](Furniture).

Wood Logs also turn up in the crates lying around the map ([Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)), rarely at industrial loot points, and in Food and Medical [supply drops](Supply-Drops). The Chainsaw itself is a rare industrial find and comes in Industrial Supplies drops; see [Melee Weapons](Melee-Weapons) for its other use.
