# Small Items

The odd items: what each one does when you press `F` with it, and which ones do nothing yet. Bigger subjects have pages of their own: [Fishing](Fishing), [Gardening](Gardening), [Furniture](Furniture), [Parachute](Parachute), [Fireworks](Fireworks), [Armour](Armour), [Explosives](Explosives), [Tools](Tools).

## Note

Size 1. Hold it and press `F`, or choose `Use` in the inventory.

- A blank note (`Note (Blank)`) opens a box to write a message, up to 256 characters. Colour codes are stripped and line breaks become spaces. A note is written **once**.
- A written note shows its message with `Close` and `Tear`. `Tear` destroys the note.

Its name shows the message: the whole text when it is 8 characters or shorter, otherwise the first 8 and `(...)`.

## Sign

Size 6. Drop the Sign, then with empty hands **tap** `F` at it to write its text (up to 128 characters; a `\` starts a new line). The text floats above the sign and can be read from 25 m away. Anyone can rewrite it the same way. Hold `F` for half a second to pick the sign up.

## Dice

Size 5, carried in both hands. Drop them with `N`: they roll 0.4 m ahead and come to rest on a random face, 1 to 6, which everyone can see.

## Locator

Size 1, made at the [Workbench](Workbench). It finds one **Mobile Phone**:

1. Drop the phone, hold the Locator and press `F` at it: *Locator synced, you can now locate the phone.*
2. From then on `F` with the Locator in hand and no phone within reach shows how far the phone is: `Distance: 12.345`.

The phone is traced lying on the ground, in a player's hands, inventory or bag, or in a box. In a vehicle trunk it is lost: *Unable to trace the mobile phone*. Syncing to another phone replaces the first.

## Stun Gun

Size 1, a melee weapon that holsters on the thigh ([Holster](Holster)). It needs a charge: hold it and press `F` at a **Tool Battery** lying on the ground. The battery is used up and the name reads `Stun Gun (Charged)`. The next punch with it in hand knocks the victim out for **60 seconds** and opens no wound; the charge is spent (`Stun Gun (Uncharged)`), and an uncharged punch does nothing but say *Out of charge*. A Stun Gun is also a Fluctuator Unit ingredient at the [Workbench](Workbench).

## Shield

Size 8. Held in your hands it stops shots arriving from within 45 degrees of your front; holstered on your back it stops shots from within 25 degrees of straight behind. It never covers the head, and covers the legs only when the shooter is crouching. See [Holster](Holster).

`F` with it in hand **plants** it: your character bends down and the shield stands on its edge half a metre in front of you, facing you. A planted shield is not solid: it stops nothing and you walk through it. Pick it up and drop it with `N` and it lies flat like any item.

## Derpification Unit

Size 1. `F` with it in hand plays one of 25 random animations, tattoo-parlour poses and gang signs. Nothing else.

## Hack Interface

Size 1, made at the [Workbench](Workbench). It cracks the keypads of [Area 69](Area-69) and nothing else: not the code hunt's lockup and not the [keypads](Keypads) players fit to their own defences. The 10 second hack, its 40 % chance and the spark that follows a failure are on [Area 69](Area-69#getting-a-code).

## Code

Size 1. A piece of the four-digit code of the San Fierro lockup; the digit is in its name, `Code (7)`. See [Code Hunt & Puzzles](Code-Hunt-and-Puzzles).

## Heart Shaped Box

A tiny bag: size 1, **6 slots**, worn on the back like any bag (`Y` to put on, `N` with empty hands to take off; see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)). One found as loot comes filled with a Dildo, Flowers, an Antiseptic Bandage and a Zorro Mask. One empty box always lies at sea near 2912, 2910 and is put back if it is carried off.

## Bodies

A death leaves a **Torso** (size 6, carried in both hands) where the player fell: tap `F` to read who it was, hold `F` to carry it, or carve one raw Meat from it with a Combat Knife. See [Death](Death#the-body).

## Camera, Spray Paint and Extinguisher

In your hands these are GTA's own camera (24 shots, no film to reload it with; its zoom is handy for scouting), spray can and fire extinguisher (100 charges each). Spraying a player does nothing to them. See [Firearms](Firearms) for the Camera's row.

## Christmas Hat

A hat like any other; see [Clothes & Hats](Clothes-and-Hats).

## No use yet

These can be picked up, carried, stored and traded, and that is all: **Keycard**, **Flare**, **Flare Gun**, **Night Vision**, **Thermal Vision**, **Pre-War Money**, **Boxing Gloves**, **Microphone**, **Pager** and **Easter Egg**. The **Bucket** does nothing on its own but is a part of the Water Purifier; see [Constructing](Constructing) and [Machines](Machines).
