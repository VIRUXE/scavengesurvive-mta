# Area 69

![Hack Interface](images/items/364_bomb.png)

Area 69 is the fenced military base in the Bone County desert, north-east of Fort Carson; the map calls it the Restricted Area. Its gates and bunker doors are locked with keypads, and every keypad has its own 4-digit code, drawn anew every time the server starts. Nothing else on the map holds as much military loot: 56 of the map's 106 military loot points lie inside the base, most of them in the bunker below ground, next to medical and industrial points. How loot points refill is on [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

![The Area 69 perimeter, watchtowers and gate](images/places/area69-gate.png)

## The doors

Walk up to a door's button and press `F` (*Activate gate* or *Activate door*): a keypad opens. The right code opens the door, and it stays open for everyone until the next server restart; from then on its button does nothing. A wrong code says *Incorrect passcode*, and a keypad takes one try every 2 seconds. Two consoles are keypads as well: their code does not open a door but shows a list of codes.

The codes come in two sectors, each listed by one console:

**Sector 01**, listed by the Control Tower console:

- Control Tower (the console itself)
- Main gate (the road gate)
- Airstrip Gate (the sliding gate by the airstrip)
- Blast Door (the two halves at the bunker entrance, with a button on either side)
- Inner Door 1 (listed, but no door takes this code)
- Inner Door 2 (the storage shutter)

**Sector 02**, listed by the Storage Watch console:

- Generator
- Passage 1 (the upper passage door)
- Passage 2 (the lower passage door)
- Catwalk
- Headquarters (two doors)
- Shaft

## Getting a code

There are 9,000 possible codes and one try every 2 seconds, so guessing is hopeless. There are two ways in:

- **The consoles.** Once a console's own code has been entered it lists the codes of its sector, and from then on `F` at the console shows the list again to anyone, no code needed. The consoles' codes are as random as the rest, so the first one has to be hacked.
- **A Hack Interface.** Hold one and press `F` at any Area 69 keypad: the display runs through random numbers for 10 seconds, then 40 % of the time the right code goes in and the door opens (or the console unlocks). Otherwise the device sparks at your feet, costs you 5 health and knocks you out for 3 seconds. The Hack Interface is used up either way, and also when you type a code yourself while holding one. Walking away from the keypad while it works cancels the hack. A Hack Interface is made at a [Workbench](Workbench) from a Data Interface and a Flux Capacitor, and it works only on these keypads: not on keypads players fit to their own [defences](Keypads) and not at the code hunt's lockup ([Code Hunt & Puzzles](Code-Hunt-and-Puzzles)).

## The shaft

By the airstrip two *Enter* buttons link the top of a shaft and its foot: press `F` at one and you come out at the other, held still for a second after the jump.

## Tips

- Doors stay open for everybody. Once someone is through, the base is open to all until the next restart; a door cannot be closed again.
- The codes are not written down anywhere else: no note, item or terminal outside the base reveals them.
