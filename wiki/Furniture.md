# Furniture

![Desk](images/items/2180_med_office5_desk_3.png) ![Table](images/items/2115_low_dinning_1.png) ![Barstool](images/items/1805_cj_barstool.png) ![Small Table](images/items/2346_cj_hifi_table.png) ![Gun Case](images/items/2046_cj_guncupboard.png) ![Cupboard](images/items/19932_mkwallovencabinet1.png)

Six items are **furniture**: the Desk, Table, Barstool and Small Table hold items on top, and the Gun Case and Cupboard display one item inside. Furniture is solid, you walk around it rather than through it, and it is **never picked up**: `F` with empty hands at a piece takes what lies on it, not the piece itself. A Crowbar takes it down; see [Constructing](Constructing).

| Furniture | Item size | Holds | How |

| Desk | 14 | any number of items | on top, 0.80 m up |
| Table | 14 | any number of items | on top, 0.80 m up |
| Barstool | 8 | any number of items | on top, 0.48 m up |
| Small Table | 10 | any number of items | on top, 0.45 m up |
| Gun Case | 12 | one item of size 5 or less | displayed lying inside |

The Desk, Table, Barstool and Small Table are built on the ground with a Screwdriver from Wood Logs and Refined Metal (the Barstool also takes a set of Clothes); recipes, build times and takedown times are on [Constructing](Constructing). The Gun Case and Cupboard have no recipe and are not found as loot today.

## Desks, tables and stools

- **Put something on it:** hold the item and press `F` at the furniture. It is set down on top, turned the way the furniture faces. It stays an ordinary world item: its label shows, anyone can take it, and it survives restarts.
- **Take something off:** with empty hands press `F` at the furniture. The highest item lying on it comes into your hands. Repeat for the next one.
- With an item in hand, `F` aimed at something already lying on the table still puts yours on the table. When your held item has a use of its own nearby (a Petrol Can at a barbecue, food at a campfire, Seeds at a plant pot), that use comes first.
- A **construction tool** (Screwdriver, Hammer and the like) pressed at furniture builds first if there are parts lying around that make a recipe; only when there is nothing to build does the tool go on the furniture.

## Gun Case and Cupboard

Each holds **one item**, shown lying inside, with its real model: a rifle on the Gun Case's rack, a bottle in the Cupboard.

- **Display:** hold the item and press `F` at the case. An item bigger than size 5 is refused with *Extra N slots required*. While the case shows an item, `F` with another item in hand does nothing.
- **Take back:** with empty hands press `F` at the case. The item comes into your hands and the case is empty again.

The displayed item is kept across restarts. The case never opens a container list like a box does; its one slot is worked only with `F`. For boxes, bags and other storage see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags).
