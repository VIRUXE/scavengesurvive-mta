# Houses & Trailers

![Shelter: a town house, the log cabins and a trailer, all enterable](images/scenes/shelter.png)

Buildings you can walk into. They are scenery that the map places in the world: you can enter them, shelter in them and fight in them, but they cannot be built, packed up or moved. For a shelter of your own see [Tent](Tent); for protecting a door see [Defences](Defences), [Keypads](Keypads) and [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks).

![A street of enterable town houses in East Beach](images/places/townhouse-street.png)

## Houses

| Kind | Designs | Placed | Notes |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| Town houses | 12 | 237 | four designs in the Los Santos style (95 placed), four Las Vegas (80), four San Fierro (62) |
| Old west house | 1 | 2 | on two farms; open doorway, no door |
| Log cabins | 1 row of 6 | 1 | six one-room cabins side by side, Flint County |

**Town houses.** Each has a modelled exterior and a hollow interior behind the entrance, so you can walk straight in from the street.

- *Doors:* every entrance has a swing door cut to the size of its opening. Walk into it to push it open. Some houses have a double door. There is no lock on a house door.
- *Windows:* the windows are real glazing: a frame with sill and trim, recessed glass and curtains behind it, instead of flat panes pasted on the wall. The glass is lit so it does not look like a dark hole at night.
- *Weather:* it does not rain indoors.
- *Contents:* a house is a bare shell with no furniture. Loot can lie inside, and what players drop or store there stays.

**Old west house.** A wooden house with a plastered interior, plank floor and ceiling and an open front doorway.

**Log cabins.** Six one-room cabins in a row beside the Flint County road. Each has its own swinging door and glazed windows.

## Trailers

Nine kinds of trailer standing in the countryside are enterable. Each has panelled rooms, glazed windows with curtains, and a door that swings: walk into it to push it open. There is no lock on them.

## The towable Airstream

A separate Airstream is a vehicle: a caravan that can be hitched, driven away and lived in.

- **Towing.** A car or pickup with the small hitch tows it: back up next to it and press the secondary fire key (`Ctrl` / `Num 0`), press it again to drop it. See [Vehicles](Vehicles). It has no engine, fuel or trunk and cannot be driven by itself.
- **Door.** Stand at the door on foot and press `F` to open or shut it. The door is solid when shut.
- **Lock.** Its lock is at the door, not on a driver's side: keys, Locksmith kits, chains and break-in tools work within reach of the door, inside or outside. A locked door can always be opened from inside, so nobody is trapped. A [Keypad](Keypads) can be fitted to the door. See [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks).
- **Defences.** Metal panels can be fitted and welded onto it, so its door cannot be forced ([Defences](Defences)). The armour makes the trailer heavier and the tow slower; a blast eats the panels first.
- **Room.** The room is about 2 m wide and 6.5 m long. Items lying on its floor ride with it while hitched, and stay where they lie when it is dropped. If the Airstream blows up, everything inside goes with it.
- **Gas Bottles** (![](images/items/20203_gas_bottle.png)). Two propane mounts sit on the A-frame at the front; a bottle is only visible while a mount holds one. With a Gas Bottle in hand press `F` at a free mount to fit it (2.5 seconds); with empty hands press `F` within 1.7 m of a filled mount to take the bottle off with its gas (2.5 seconds). A trailer found in the world starts with none, one or both bottles, holding 0.5 to 11 kg each. Nothing inside burns that gas yet: a stove and a heater are planned, so for now the mounts only carry bottles, for welding ([Vehicle Armour](Vehicle-Armour)) or trade.
- **Abandoned.** Like any vehicle, an Airstream nobody has touched for a while is removed. Opening its door or standing in it counts as use.
