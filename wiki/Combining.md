# Combining

Combining makes a new item out of two items you carry, without a tool bench. It is the quickest of the three ways to craft; the other two are [Constructing](Constructing) (on the ground) and the [Workbench](Workbench). The in-game `/recipes` window lists all three kinds.

## How to combine

1. Hold one of the two items in your hands.
2. Open the inventory (`H`) and open the options of the other item.
3. Choose **Combine**. If the pair is a recipe, the result is made at once; if not, you are told there is no recipe.

A recipe takes exactly two items. Ingredients marked *kept* stay with you; the others are used up. When the held item is used up, the result ends up in your hands; when it is kept, the result goes into the container the other item was in.

## Recipes

| Result | Ingredients | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Parachute Bag | Parachute, Combat Knife (kept) | |
| Empty Molotov | Bottle, Dirty Bandage | Fill it with petrol to make a Molotov, see below |
| Dirty Bandage | Clothes, Combat Knife (kept) | Cut up a spare set of [clothes](Clothes-and-Hats) |
| Lock and Chain | Lock and Chain, Locksmith Kit | Cuts the lock so it can be used, see [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks); a lock that is already cut is refused |

## Filling a Molotov

An Empty Molotov is not filled by combining: drop it, hold a [Petrol Can](Fuel) with petrol in it and press `F` at the bottle, and 0.5 litres make it a throwable Molotov. See [Explosives](Explosives#molotov).

## Other crafting

- More complex items (electronics, bombs, motors, panels) are made at a [Workbench](Workbench).
- Furniture, machines, the Campfire and Workbench itself are built on the ground, see [Constructing](Constructing).
- Phone-remote charges are not combined with the phone either: the two are synced with `F`, see [Explosives](Explosives).
