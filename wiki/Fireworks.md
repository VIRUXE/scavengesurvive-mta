# Fireworks

![Fireworks](images/items/2039_ammo_box_s1.png) ![Lighter](images/items/19998_cutscenelighterfl.png)

A **Fireworks** box (size 2) is a firework you set off with a **Lighter**. It is also an ingredient: a Fireworks box and a Power Supply make an IED at the [Workbench](Workbench); see [Explosives](Explosives).

## Setting one off

1. Drop the Fireworks box on the ground.
2. Hold a Lighter and press `F` at the box. Your character bends down to light the fuse. The Lighter is not used up.
3. Six seconds later a rocket climbs about 10 m straight up from the box and bursts.

The burst comes in waves: the rocket bursts into four flashes, each flash flies off and bursts into three sparks that fly about 50 m out and down, and each spark ends in four small fires that die where they land. The flight is a little different for every player watching.

## Again

The box is not used up: it stays where it lies and can be lit again. Only one firework can be lit every 3 seconds across the whole server; a Lighter pressed at a box within 3 seconds of the last launch does nothing, so wait and try again.

Lighting the box needs the Lighter *at the box*. A Lighter pressed with no Fireworks box within reach does what it usually does: it lights a [campfire or barbecue](Campfire-and-BBQ) or builds a campfire from three Wood Logs ([Constructing](Constructing)).
