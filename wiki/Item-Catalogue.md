# Item Catalogue

Every item in the game, grouped by kind. Each row shows the item's render and its [size](Item-Sizes), which is the number of inventory slots it takes up.

Where to read more:

- Weapons: [Firearms](Firearms), [Melee Weapons](Melee-Weapons), [Explosives](Explosives)
- [Ammunition](Ammunition) and its calibres
- Food and drinks: [Food & Drink](Food-and-Drink)
- Bags and containers: [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)
- Healing: [Medical](Medical) and [Drugs](Drugs)
- Building: [Defences](Defences), [Keypads](Keypads), [Tent](Tent), [Constructing](Constructing), [Workbench](Workbench)
- Cooking and fire: [Campfire & BBQ](Campfire-and-BBQ)
- Clothing and headgear: [Clothes & Hats](Clothes-and-Hats)
- Tools and parts: [Tools](Tools), [Scrap](Scrap), [Machines](Machines)

Several different items can share a name (there are, for example, three kinds of Pizza and two Metal Panels). Their sizes and uses can differ, so check the render and the page for the kind of item you mean.
