# Parachute

![Parachute](images/items/371_gun_para.png)

A **Parachute** (size 6) is worn on your back and lets you jump from a height, a roof or an aircraft and float down.

## Putting it on

Hold the Parachute and press `Y` (or `F`). It leaves your hands and goes on your back, and you are told *Equipped*. Two rules:

- you cannot put it on while you wear a bag (*You can't wear a parachute while you are wearing a bag*), and
- while the parachute is on, you cannot put a bag on (*You can't wear a bag while you are wearing a parachute*).

So a parachute and a bag share the one place on your back; take one off before the other goes on. The parachute does not show in the inventory's gear tiles.

## Taking it off

With **empty hands**, press `N`. The Parachute comes off your back into your hands. It can then be dropped, put away or handed over like any item, and used again later.

## Using it

Jump. Once you are in the air the parachute is your character's parachute as in GTA: open it and steer as usual. When it has carried you to the ground it is spent: the item is gone from your back and you have nothing to take off.

A parachute taken off on the ground is not spent, and neither is one you lose by dying: a death drops it on the ground with everything else you carried ([Inventory](Inventory)). Logging out with it on keeps it on your back for the next time you play.

## Parachute Bag

A Parachute combined with a Combat Knife (the knife is kept) makes a **Parachute Bag**, a 15-slot bag worn like any other bag; see [Combining](Combining) and [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags). The parachute is used up in the making, so decide first whether you want the jump or the slots.
