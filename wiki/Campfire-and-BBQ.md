# Campfire & BBQ

![A camp at dusk: the pitched tent, a campfire and a BBQ, a backpack and crates](images/scenes/camp.png)

Campfires and barbecues cook food. Food that can be cooked (the Food table on [Food & Drink](Food-and-Drink) marks which) is worth less when eaten raw, and some raw food can make you ill (see [Infection](Infection)). Cooked food shows `(Cooked)` in its name.

![Campfire](images/items/19632_firewood1.png) ![Barbecue](images/items/19831_barbeque1.png) ![Wood Log](images/items/19793_firewoodlog1.png)

## Campfire

**Building:** drop 3 Wood Logs together on the ground, hold a Lighter and press `F` (5 seconds); see [Constructing](Constructing). Wood Logs come from felling trees with a Chainsaw. No petrol is needed.

**Cooking:** hold a food item and press `F` at the fire. The food is placed on it and cooks for 1 minute. Once cooked, empty hands take it off the fire (not before it finishes). One food at a time cooks on a campfire.

<!-- shot:shot-campfire-cooking -->

**Burning out:** a campfire burns for 10 minutes. In rain it burns for only 2 minutes, and 60 % of the time it fizzles out at once. When it goes out it leaves 3 Burnt Logs (or 3 Wood Logs if the rain put it out right away). A campfire cannot be picked up.

## Barbecue (BBQ)

![BBQ](images/items/19831_barbeque1.png)

The BBQ is an item you carry (size 6) and place anywhere.

1. **Fuel it.** Hold a Petrol Can with petrol in it and press `F` at the BBQ. 0.6 litres go in for 10 cooks.
2. **Load it.** Hold food and press `F` at the BBQ. It has two grill spots, one on each side. Food that is already cooked is refused.
3. **Light it.** Hold a Lighter and press `F` at the BBQ. Everything on the grill cooks in 30 seconds and uses one cook of fuel.

An unlit BBQ gives its food back to empty hands, and then the BBQ itself can be picked up. While it is cooking, nothing on the grill can be taken. A BBQ without fuel tells you it needs fuel from a Petrol Can.


A Campfire's and BBQ's contents survive server restarts.
