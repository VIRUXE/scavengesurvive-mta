# Surrender & Disarm

Not every meeting has to end in a shootout. You can raise your hands or drop everything to show you are no threat, and a player who is knocked out or cuffed can be relieved of what they hold. This page covers the hands-up pose, `/dropall`, disarming and Handcuffs.

## Hands up

Hold **Sprint** and **Crouch** together (`Space` and `C` by default) to put your hands up. Do the same again to lower them.

While your hands are up:

- you cannot walk, jump or enter a vehicle;
- you cannot pick up or use items, open your inventory or containers, or take anything out of one;

Knocking you out, killing you, putting you in a vehicle or handcuffing you also ends the pose. Hands up cannot be started while you are in a vehicle, unconscious or handcuffed.

`/dropall` is the full surrender: it drops everything you carry, wear and hold on the ground around you, raises your hands and leaves you alive. See [Inventory](Inventory).

A standing character can also sit: hold **Crouch** for a moment while standing still. Sprint, jump or crouch again to stand up.

## Disarming

An unconscious player can be disarmed. Stand next to them (within 2 m) with **empty hands** and press the interact key (`F`). The item in their hands moves into yours and you see `Disarmed player`. See [Health](Health) for what knocks a player out.

Only the item in their hands is taken. Their holster, bag and inventory stay on them.

## Handcuffs

![Handcuffs](images/items/19418_handcuffs01.png)

Handcuffs (size 1) let you restrain another player. You can only cuff someone who holds no item and has no weapon drawn, is not in a vehicle and is not on staff duty, and you cannot start from inside a vehicle yourself.

- **Cuffing:** with the Handcuffs in your hands, stand within 2 m of the player and hold `F`. A `Handcuffing` bar runs for 3 seconds while your character works the cuffs. At the end they are cuffed, the Handcuffs are used up and you see *Handcuffed player*.
- **Uncuffing:** with empty hands and no weapon drawn, hold `F` next to a cuffed player for 3 seconds (`Removing handcuffs`). You see *Removed handcuffs* and a pair of Handcuffs is in your hands.

The hold is cancelled if you let go of `F`, move more than a step (0.8 m) or change what you hold, if the target moves more than 2.5 m away, gets into a vehicle, picks something up or draws a weapon, or if either of you dies or leaves. Only one person can be cuffing a given player at a time.

A cuffed player cannot fire, aim, jump, sprint, get into a vehicle (as driver or passenger), switch weapon or use the action key, and cannot pick up or use items, open the inventory or a container, take anything out of one, put their hands up or sit. They can still walk. Cuffing a player whose hands are up lowers them. The cuffs come off only when another player removes them.

See also [Aimshout](Aimshout), [Health](Health) and [Death](Death).
