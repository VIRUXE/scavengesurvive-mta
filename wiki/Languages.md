# Languages

The interface comes in ten languages. Your choice is saved on your account and every text the server sends you is shown in it.

| Code | Language |
|:--|:--|
| `en` | English |
| `es` | Español |
| `fr` | Français |
| `pt` | Português (Portugal) |
| `ptbr` | Português (Brasil) |
| `ru` | Русский |
| `ro` | Română |
| `id` | Bahasa Indonesia |
| `bhs` | Bosanski / Hrvatski / Srpski |
| `cs` | Čeština |

## Choosing one

The first time you connect, before the register dialog, a window titled **Language** lists the ten under their own names (*Choose your language / Escolhe o teu idioma*). Pick one and press **Continue**, double-click it, or press `Enter`; the register dialog then opens in that language. The window appears to every account that has no language yet and never again afterwards. See [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).

<!-- shot:shot-login-language -->

Later, type `/lang <code>` with a code from the table: `/lang es` answers *Language set to es.* Typing it without a code, or with a wrong one, prints the usage line with every valid code and the language you are using now. You have to be logged in. The change is saved on your account, so it holds for your next character and your next login; see [Commands](Commands).

## What is translated

- The whole interface: dialogs, the inventory, menus, action texts, tips, the key list, the welcome text and the server's chat notices. All ten languages have every one of these texts.
- The names of **items** and their types, of **ammunition** kinds and **calibres**, of **liquids** in a bottle or can and of **seeds**, together with the labels, messages and actions of the item windows: an `M9 (10/7, 9mm, Hollow Point)` reads in your language, and so does the label on a Bottle of water on the ground. Supply drop types in the chat announcement are translated as well.

Anything another player writes stays as written: chat lines, notes, signs, the names of players and of Discord users. Model names, brands and acronyms (M9, AK-47, S.W.A.T.) are the same in every language.

## English fallback

A text that has no translation yet in your language is shown in English, and one whose translation could not take its numbers is shown in English too, so you never see a broken line. Most of the texts still missing are model names and brands; the number varies by language, a few dozen out of about nine hundred item texts.

See also [Account & Login](Account-and-Login), [Commands](Commands) and [Joining the Server](Joining-the-Server).
