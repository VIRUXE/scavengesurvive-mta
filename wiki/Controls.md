# Controls

The keys below are the defaults. The item keys (`F`, `N`, `Y`, `H`, `R`) are ordinary MTA key binds, so you can change them in MTA's key-bind settings (the commands behind them are `interact`, `dropitem`, `putaway`, `inventory` and `reloadweapon`). The on-screen key hints follow your bindings for those five. Actions that use GTA's own controls (sit, hands up, lock, hitch) follow whatever you set for those controls.

## On foot

| Action | Key | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Interact | `F` | Context sensitive, see the rows below. |
| Pick up | `F` (tap) | Picks up the nearest item within 2 m. Hold `F` for 500 ms to pick up a bag, box, sign, body or plant pot, which a tap opens, reads or inspects instead; the same hold picks up a Welder or a gas cylinder lying by one, which a tap uses instead (see [Vehicle Armour](Vehicle-Armour)). `F` is also GTA's enter-vehicle key: it boards a vehicle only when there is nothing to use, pick up or open where you stand. |
| Signs, bodies, plant pots | `F` (tap) | With empty hands: write on a sign, read who a body was, see a plant pot's state. See [Small Items](Small-Items) and [Gardening](Gardening). |
| Eat, drink, use, wear | `F` | With food, a drink, clothes, a wearable or similar in your hands. Treatments (bandage, medkit, pills...) run while `F` is held; letting go cancels. |
| Open a held bag | `F` | With a bag or safebox in your hands. A bag lying on the ground opens on a tap and is picked up on a hold. |
| Machines | `F` | Tap opens the machine's container, hold opens its menu. See [Machines](Machines). |
| Vehicle tools, trunk | `F` | With a vehicle tool in hand next to a vehicle, or standing behind a vehicle for its trunk. See [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks). |
| Bonnet, battery | `F` | With empty hands at the front of a vehicle: a tap opens or shuts the bonnet, a hold (500 ms) takes the battery out. With a Car Battery in hand, `F` fits it. See [Vehicles](Vehicles). |
| Airstream door, gas mounts | `F` | At the trailer's door opens or closes it. At the A-frame: a Gas Bottle in hand goes onto a mount, empty hands take one off. See [Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers). |
| Vehicle armour | `F` | With a defence in hand at the Airstream: starts attaching it; `F` again fixes it where the ghost lies, `N` cancels. With the Welder's torch: welds; with an Angle Grinder: grinds. See [Vehicle Armour](Vehicle-Armour). |
| Fishing | `F` (hold) | With a fishing rod: the longer you hold, the farther the cast (up to 10 s). Press `F` again to reel in. See [Fishing](Fishing). |
| Ladders, ziplines | `F` | See [Ladders & Ziplines](Ladders-and-Ziplines). |
| Drop / give | `N` (tap) | Drops what you hold; gives it to a player standing within 1.5 m of you instead. Also cancels attaching a defence and puts the Welder's torch back. |
| Unload a firearm | `N` (hold 300 ms) | Takes the magazine out of the firearm in your hands. |
| Take off bag or parachute | `N` | With empty hands: takes off a worn parachute first, otherwise your bag. See [Parachute](Parachute). |
| Put away / draw | `Y` | Puts away what you hold, or draws the item from your holster. See [Holster](Holster). |
| Inventory | `H` | Opens and closes the [inventory](Inventory). |
| Reload | `R` | Reloads the firearm in your hands. |
| Move a placed item | `LALT` | While nudging an item you just placed, frees the mouse for the tweak pad (the Move button hides it again). Enter or Done finishes; dropping, putting away or opening the inventory finishes it too. |

## In a vehicle

| Action | Key | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Engine on / off | `Y` | Driver only. A tap with the engine off turns the key to ACC (dashboard on) or back off; holding `Y` for 0.3 s cranks the engine; pressing it while the engine runs stops it. The engine needs fuel, a charged battery and a vehicle that is not wrecked, see [Vehicles](Vehicles). |
| Lights on / off | `N` | Any seat. |
| Radio | `R` | Opens the [radio](Radio) panel. If you hold a reloadable firearm, hold `R` for 600 ms instead (a tap reloads). |
| Lock / unlock doors | `2` (GTA's "sub mission" control) or `/lock` | From inside the vehicle, any seat. |
| Hitch / unhitch a trailer | `LCtrl` / `Num 0` (GTA's "vehicle secondary fire") | Driver of a vehicle with a hitch, when the key list shows `Hitch trailer` or `Unhitch trailer`. |
| Fire the gun | Vehicle fire | Driver of an armed vehicle; mind the heat bar, see [Vehicles](Vehicles). |

## Poses and combat

| Action | Key | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Sit | Hold crouch for 0.8 s while standing still | A short tap stays a normal crouch. |
| Stand up | Crouch, sprint or jump | While sitting. |
| Hands up / surrender | Sprint + crouch | Press again to lower your hands. See [Surrender & Disarm](Surrender-and-Disarm). |
| Aimshout | Aim + look behind | In a car: look behind + horn. Shouts your line, set with `/aimshout`. See [Aimshout](Aimshout). |

## Chat and windows

| Action | Key | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Chat | `T` | MTA's chat key; see [Chat](Chat) and [Commands](Commands). |
| Voice | MTA's push-to-talk key | Heard within 40 m and by your chat mode's audience, see [Chat](Chat). |
| Dialogs | `Enter` / `Escape` | The left and right button of any list or message box; `Escape` also closes the radio panel. See [HUD](HUD). |

## Rebinding

- `F`, `N`, `Y`, `H` and `R` can be rebound in MTA's key-bind settings. If you have already bound one of those commands, the game keeps your binding. Inside a vehicle the engine and lights follow the `Y` and `N` bindings.
- Sit, hands up, aimshout, lock and hitch use GTA's controls (crouch, sprint, jump, aim, look behind, horn, "sub mission", "vehicle secondary fire"), so rebinding those controls in MTA's settings changes them here too.
- The radio key inside a vehicle is always `R`.
