# FAQ

Short answers to the questions new players ask, with the page that has the long one. If you are brand new, read [Getting Started](Getting-Started) first.

## Account and settings

**How do I change my language?** `/lang <code>` with `en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ptbr`, `ru`, `ro`, `id`, `bhs` or `cs`; the first join also asks in a window. Untranslated lines show in English. See [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).

**How do I change my password?** `/changepass <old> <new>`; the new one is 4 to 32 characters. See [Commands](Commands).

**Can I rebind the keys?** Yes: `F`, `N`, `Y`, `H` and `R` are ordinary key binds (`interact`, `dropitem`, `putaway`, `inventory`, `reloadweapon`) and the on-screen key list follows your bindings. Sit, hands up, aimshout, lock and hitch use GTA's own controls, so rebinding those changes them too. See [Controls](Controls).

**Why was I kicked?** Most often ping: more than 400 ms (500 ms while 10 or fewer players are online) for 10 seconds in a row. Minimising or unfocusing the game for more than 60 seconds kicks as well, and so does moving a parked vehicle from outside or flooding the server with requests. See [Fair Play](Fair-Play).

**Why am I "Not whitelisted"?** The staff have turned the whitelist on; unlisted players are disconnected when the countdown ends. See [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).

## Playing

**How do I open the inventory?** `H`. Six slots, the Health Status panel and six gear tiles. See [Inventory](Inventory).

**Is there permadeath?** Yes. One character; at 0 health everything you held, wore and carried drops around a body and your next character starts on a beach with nothing but an Antiseptic Bandage. Skills die with the character; the account, password and language stay. See [Death](Death).

**Can I log out to escape a fight?** No. Quitting within 30 seconds of being hit by a player who is still online kills your character where it stood, exactly like a death. See [Combat Log](Combat-Log).

**Do my items survive a server restart?** Yes. Everything in the world, in containers, in trunks, in tents and on your character is kept; the world comes back as it was. See [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

**I spawned with nothing. Where is my bag?** New characters get no bag: your first character holds a Combat Knife, every one after a death an Antiseptic Bandage, and that is all. Bags are loot; hold one and press `Y` to wear it. See [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags).

**I caught a fish and cannot put it away.** A Fish is size 9 and never fits the 6-slot inventory. Carry it in your hands, or put it in a bag of nine slots or more (Backpack, Medium Bag, Large Backpack, Parachute Bag, Travel Rucksack), a trunk or a box. See [Fishing](Fishing) and [Item Sizes](Item-Sizes).

**Where is my armour bar?** There is none on the HUD. A worn vest shows its points only in the inventory's `TORSO` tile, `ARMOUR (73)`. See [Armour](Armour).

**The key list does not mention the trunk, repairs, the engine or the lights.** It never does: `F` at the rear of a vehicle opens the trunk and `F` with a tool at the bonnet repairs all the same, and `Y` and `N` work the engine and lights from the driver's seat. See [Controls](Controls) and [HUD](HUD).

**I was muted "from global chat" but cannot talk at all.** A mute blocks every chat mode and voice, not only global, for its whole length; three lines within one second earns 30 seconds of it. See [Chat](Chat) and [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans).

## Items and crafting

**Does the Chainsaw need petrol to fell trees?** No. Felling uses no petrol at all; the saw only burns petrol when it is swung as a weapon. See [Trees & Wood](Trees-and-Wood).

**What does the Hack Interface open?** Only the keypads of Area 69. It does nothing at the code hunt's lockup or at keypads players fit to their own defences. See [Area 69](Area-69).

**Does the Plot Pole protect my base?** No. It only tells everyone who comes within 20 m that they are entering or leaving claimed territory; it stops nobody from building, taking down or picking anything up. The defences do the protecting. See [Defences](Defences).

**What does the Water Purifier take?** Bottles of sea water, and nothing fills a bottle with sea water today, so the machine stands idle. See [Machines](Machines).

**How do I get a Gun Case or a Cupboard?** You do not, for now: neither has a recipe and neither turns up as loot. See [Furniture](Furniture).

**What do the gas bottles on the Airstream do?** Nothing yet: the two mounts only carry Gas Bottles. A stove and a heater are planned; until then take a bottle off and use it for welding. See [Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers).

**What does the Scrap Machine give?** One Scrap Metal per point of an item's scrap value, at most 11 per cook, and an item with no scrap value is destroyed. The Refining Machine turns each Scrap Metal into exactly one Refined Metal. See [Scrap](Scrap) and [Machines](Machines).

**Someone carried off the workbench / the scrapyard machines.** The fixed workbenches, the scrapyard machines and the other fixed items are put back when the server restarts, not before. You can build your own. See [World & Zones](World-and-Zones) and [Workbench](Workbench).

## The world

**Why does no loot appear where I stand?** Loot never spawns within 150 m of a player; a point only rolls while someone is 150 to 400 m away, and then waits about an hour before it rolls again. See [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

**Why is there so little loot lately?** Everything alive counts against each zone's capacity: items on the ground, in boxes, in trunks and in players' bags. A big stockpile slows the respawn for the whole server. See [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

**How long does loot stay on the ground?** 120 minutes if nobody touches it, a dead player's items included. Picking an item up resets that. See [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

**My parked car disappeared.** A vehicle driven away from its spawn point and not used for 3 days is removed with everything in its trunk. Getting in, opening the trunk or hitching it counts as use. A wrecked vehicle vanishes 30 seconds after it blows up. See [Vehicles](Vehicles) and [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn).

**Where do I spawn?** At one of 14 spots at the water's edge, never the same one twice in a row. The map is on [Death](Death).

## Staff and rules

**How do I report someone?** `/report <name> [reason]`, `/report near` (the closest player within 100 m) or `/report killer` (whoever last killed you); `/report` alone opens a menu. `/bug` files a bug report. See [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans).

**How do I appeal a ban?** Contact a Moderator or above (`/admins` lists them, or the community's Discord) with the name you were banned under, the date and the reason shown. A shared-address catch is lifted and reissued against the right person. See [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans).

**What gets me banned automatically?** A jetpack, swimming where there is no water, and a vehicle with more health than any repair can give. Position jumps on foot are undone and reported instead. See [Fair Play](Fair-Play).
