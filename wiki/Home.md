# Scavenge & Survive Reborn

Scavenge & Survive is a PvP survival game on a decayed San Andreas, where supplies are scarce and everyone is willing to kill for the same things. You have one character; everything you own is a physical item that can be dropped, found or stolen, wounds bleed, and when you die you drop it all.

## The world

The whole of San Andreas is open, divided into seven zones that decide how much loot each region holds; there are no safe areas. See [World & Zones](World-and-Zones) for the zones, the loot and the weather, and [Loot & Respawn](Loot-and-Respawn) for how the world refills.

## What changed from the original

This is a port of Southclaws' Scavenge & Survive for SA-MP, and the game is the same game. The world now lives in a database, so nothing is lost on a restart and loot comes back through a scarcity budget instead of a 5-hour reset. Cars have batteries and starter motors, a Bobcat comes apart, an Airstream can be towed and armoured, and houses, trailers and cabins can be entered. The full list, with what was dropped, is on [Changes from the Original](Changes-from-the-Original).
