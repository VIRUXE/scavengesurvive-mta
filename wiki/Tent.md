# Tent

![A camp at dusk: the pitched tent, a campfire and a BBQ, a backpack and crates](images/scenes/camp.png)

![Tent Pack](images/items/1279_craigpackage.png)

A tent is a camouflaged shelter with its own 16-slot storage. It is carried as a **Tent Pack** (size 6) and pitched where you want it.

## Pitching

1. Drop the Tent Pack on the ground where the tent should stand.
2. Hold a **Hammer** and press `F` near the pack. A bar runs for 3 seconds ("Pitching tent") and the pack becomes a tent: four camouflage sheets leaning together over the pack, with a pole at each end.

You must stay within about 3 m of the pack and keep the Hammer in your hands until the bar finishes.

## Storage

Press `F` at a pitched tent to open its container. It holds 16 slots (see [Item Sizes](Item-Sizes)). What you store in it is kept across server restarts, and the tent itself stays where you pitched it. It does not hold items lying on the ground around it, only what you place in it.

## Packing it up

Hold a **Crowbar** and press `F` at the tent. After 3 seconds ("Packing tent") it becomes a Tent Pack again. The tent has to be empty first; otherwise you are told that the tent must be empty to pack it away.

## Tips

- The sheets are camouflage coloured, so a tent in bushes or trees is easy to miss.
- Put a [campfire or barbecue](Campfire-and-BBQ) beside it for a camp.
- A tent has no lock, so anyone who finds it can open it.
