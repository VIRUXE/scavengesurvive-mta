# Tent

![Tent Pack](images/items/1279_craigpackage.png)

Tents are disabled. The **Tent Pack** no longer turns up as loot or in supply drops and a Hammer no longer pitches it, and every tent and Tent Pack was taken out of the world on 2026-11-19. What lay in the tents was left on the ground where it was.

For a shelter, see [Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers); to keep things, see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags).
