# Clothes & Hats

Your character wears four kinds of gear on top of their skin. Each has its own slot, shown as a tile on the right of the inventory ([Inventory](Inventory)).

| Slot | Tile | Items |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Head | `HEAD` | hats and helmets |
| Face | `FACE` | masks and bandanas |
| Torso | `TORSO` | Armour |
| Back | `BACK` | bags (see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)) |

## Putting on and taking off

- Hold the item and press the interact key (`F`), or
- choose `Wear` on it in the inventory list, or
- click the matching empty tile while holding it.

To take something off, click its tile in the inventory. It goes into your hands, or into your inventory if your hands are full (and the inventory has room). Putting something on a filled slot swaps the old item out. Bags are different: press `Y` to put one on and `N` with empty hands to take it off.

### Bare heads under a hat

Many skins carry their own headgear in the clothes themselves: the sheriffs, firefighters, police, SWAT, army, bikers, golfers, pilot, pizza and burger outfits and others. Putting a hat on top of those would show both. So while you wear a hat, such a skin is shown with its headgear removed and a bare head, and the hat sits on that head. This is done for 22 skins; the bare heads of three police skins are plainer than the rest. Take the hat off and you look as before. Your character's skin does not change: the swap only affects how players see you, and it is shown to everyone around you.

A worn hat cuts the chance of a headshot knocking you out by 60 %.

## Armour

The `TORSO` slot takes Armour, a vest with 25 to 99 points that soaks up part of every chest shot until it is spent; see [Armour](Armour).

## Headshots knock gear off

A headshot knocks your hat and mask off. They fall to the ground at your feet and can be picked up again.

## Clothes

A Clothes item holds a skin (a set of clothes). Using one changes your character's look:

1. Hold the item and press `F`, or choose `Wear` on it in the inventory. A 3 second bar fills; moving cancels it.
2. Your skin changes, and the item now holds the clothes you took off.

You can only use Clothes of your own gender (`Male ...` or `Female ...` in the item name; the name also shows the type, such as Civilian, Cop or Military). Your skin is saved with your character.

The server has 29 sets of clothes (15 male, 14 female): several civilian looks plus Mechanic, Biker, Cop, Military, S.W.A.T., Triad, Country, Indian, Business and a few rare ones. A new character spawns in one of them, picked at random between male and female and weighted towards the civilian looks, so the rarer ones (S.W.A.T., Military, Business) seldom appear. Every skin wears hats, masks, Armour and bags correctly.

Clothes items also turn up as loot. Each one holds one of these 29 sets, chosen when it is created. When you die, a Clothes item holding the skin you died in lies next to your body, so a killer can take your look. See also [Item Catalogue](Item-Catalogue).
