# Tools

Tools are items you hold to build, take down and repair things. Hold the tool and press `F` at the target; most jobs are a held `F` with a progress bar. The [Construction skill](Skills) shortens building and removal times.

![Hammer](images/items/18635_gtasahammer1.png) ![Wrench](images/items/18633_gtasawrench1.png) ![Screwdriver](images/items/18644_screwdriver1.png) ![Crowbar](images/items/18634_gtasacrowbar1.png) ![Sledgehammer](images/items/19631_sledgehammer1.png) ![Lighter](images/items/19998_cutscenelighterfl.png)

| Tool | Size | Uses |
|:--|--:|:--|
| Hammer | 2 | Lays a [defence](Defences) flat (a floor or ramp). Pitches a [tent](Tent) (3 s). Builds a Workbench from parts on the ground, and is the tool for the Metal Panel and Gas Cylinder [Workbench](Workbench) recipes. Repairs vehicles. |
| Screwdriver | 1 | Stands a [defence](Defences) upright (a wall). Builds furniture, machines and a Plot Pole from parts on the ground, copies keys, and is the tool for most [Workbench](Workbench) recipes (explosives, keypads, motors). Repairs vehicles. |
| Wrench | 2 | Repairs vehicles. |
| Crowbar | 2 | Takes down [defences](Defences) (from behind, 10 s), packs up an empty [tent](Tent) (3 s), and deconstructs things built from ground parts (25 s for the Workbench and the machines). Breaks open vehicle locks. |
| Sledgehammer | 7 | Builds a Locker and a Large Frame from parts on the ground. Also a heavy [melee weapon](Melee-Weapons). |
| Lighter | 1 | Lights a [campfire](Campfire-and-BBQ) (built from 3 Wood Logs), a barbecue, and a [Fireworks](Fireworks) box. |
| Locator | 1 | Synced to a Mobile Phone with `F`; a second `F` shows the distance to the phone. See [Small Items](Small-Items). |

## Welder and welding gas

![Welder](images/items/20202_welder.png) ![Generator](images/items/20204_generator.png) ![Gas Bottle](images/items/20203_gas_bottle.png) ![Gas Cylinder](images/items/19816_oxygencylinder1.png)

The **Welder** (size 6) welds metal defences onto the towable Airstream trailer; see [Vehicle Armour](Vehicle-Armour). It is carried in both hands and works lying on the ground:

- Lay it down. With empty hands, tap `F` at it to take its torch; hold `F` to pick the Welder up instead. `N`, or `F` anywhere but at a vehicle that takes armour, puts the torch back on the Welder.
- It needs power from a fuelled **Generator** (size 8, also two-handed) within 8 m, see [Machines](Machines), and welding gas: a **Gas Cylinder** or **Gas Bottle** lying within 2.5 m of the Welder, hooked up with a tap of `F` at it (another tap unhooks it).

| Gas | Size | Full | As found |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| Gas Cylinder | 5 | 20 kg | full |
| Gas Bottle | 5 | 11 kg | 0.5 to 11 kg, shown in its name: `Gas Bottle (6.3/11 kg)` |

A weld burns **0.44 kg of gas per hit point the defence type can take**, whatever damage the panel already carries, so 2.2 kg for a 5-point panel; with too little gas hooked up nothing is welded and you are told so. The Gas Cylinder is also a part of the machines and the Plot Pole ([Constructing](Constructing)).

A Gas Bottle can also ride on the two mounts of the towable Airstream's A-frame, where it is stored, not used; see [Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers).
