# Ladders & Ziplines

Some tall structures have a climbable ladder and some high places have a zipline. Both are used with the interact key (`F`) while you stand close; the key list in the corner shows *Climb ladder* or *Grab zipline* when one is in reach, and *Let go* while you are on one.

## Ladders

Stand within 1 m of the foot of the ladder, anywhere from just below its base to just above its top, and press `F`. You snap onto the ladder facing it.

- `W` climbs, `S` climbs down (more slowly), and with no key you hold your place.
- `F` again lets go.
- Climbing past either end for more than a second steps you off, forward at the top.

There are 14 ladders:

| Where | Ladders |
|:--|:--|
| Easter Bay Chemicals, Flint County (the chemical plant) | Nine: seven of 10 to 25 m and two of almost 100 m up its tallest structure |
| Market, Los Santos | One of 15 m |
| Chinatown, San Fierro | One of 10 m |
| Easter Bay Airport, San Fierro | Three of 12 m: one on the south-west side of the airport and two at the water's edge on its east side |

The ladders and passages that belong to the puzzles (the ladder between the caves and Ganton, for example) are buttons instead, see [Code Hunt & Puzzles](Code-Hunt-and-Puzzles).

## Ziplines

A zipline is a rope between two anchors, drawn as a dark line. Stand within 2 m of the start anchor, or anywhere beneath the rope, and press `F`: you hang about 1.2 m below it and slide towards the far end, getting faster as you go up to a top speed.

- `F` lets go early and gives you a small push forwards.
- You are let go automatically near the end (within 6 m of the far anchor) or if you drift more than 5 m off the line.
- Grabbing a ladder while riding lets go of the rope. Dying also ends the ride.
- Ropes only go one way, from start to end.

There are 13 ziplines:

| Where | Lines |
|:--|:--|
| Las Colinas, Los Santos | Three short lines between the roofs: 37, 48 and 97 m |
| Paradiso, San Fierro | One of 48 m |
| Santa Flora, San Fierro | One of 70 m |
| Chinatown, San Fierro | Two, of 35 and 82 m |
| Calton Heights, San Fierro | Two: 107 m within the district and 171 m down into Downtown |
| Financial district, San Fierro | Four long lines from a rooftop almost 300 m up: 380 m down into Downtown, 260 m and 171 m to lower roofs in the district, and 527 m eastwards out of it |

Anchors sometimes hang in the air beside a roof, so look for the rope.
