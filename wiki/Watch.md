# Watch

The watch is the black oval at the bottom left of your screen. It shows three things, top to bottom:

| Line | Shows |
|:--|:--|
| Bearing | The direction you face (or your vehicle faces), as a compass heading in degrees: `0 DEG` is north, `90 DEG` east, `180 DEG` south, `270 DEG` west. |
| Time | The in-game clock, 24-hour, as `HH:MM`. |
| Frequency | The [radio](Radio) frequency you are tuned to, for example `98.12`. |

The text is white. It turns red while your combat window is open after another player wounds you; see [Combat Log](Combat-Log).

## Using it

- The bearing lets you give and follow directions ("walk 270 degrees from the church") without a map.
- The frequency lets you check you are on the same channel as your group without opening the radio panel.

The watch needs no item: everyone always has one.
