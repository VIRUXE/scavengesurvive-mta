# Holster

The holster is a single slot on your character for one weapon. It holds a weapon without using your hands, and the weapon is visible on your body to other players.

![M9](images/items/346_colt45.png) ![Shield](images/items/18637_policeshield1.png)

## Using it

- **Holster:** hold a holsterable weapon and press `Y`. A short animation plays and your hands become empty. If you already had a weapon holstered, the two swap.
- **Draw:** with empty hands, press `Y`. The weapon goes back into your hands.
- You can also click the `HOLSTER` tile in the inventory ([Inventory](Inventory)), or choose `Wear` on the weapon.

`Y` does nothing while you are in a vehicle (there it switches the engine), knocked out, handcuffed or on staff duty, and holstering or drawing can only be repeated once a second.

Only one weapon fits. A bag is a separate slot, so you can carry a holstered weapon and wear a bag at once.

## Where each weapon sits

Thigh weapons take 0.3 seconds to holster or draw. Weapons on the back take 0.8 seconds (the Spade and Sword 0.6 seconds).

| Position | Weapons |
|:--|:--|
| Thigh | M9, M9 SD, Desert Eagle, Sawnoff, Mac 10, Tec 9, Stun Gun, Baton, Dog's Breath |
| Back | Shotgun, Spas 12, MP5, WASR-3, M16, Rifle, Sniper, AK-47, M77-RM, Model 70, The Len-Knocks, RPG, Heatseeker, Spade, Sword, Shield |

See [Firearms](Firearms) and [Melee Weapons](Melee-Weapons) for the weapons themselves.

## The Shield

A holstered Shield rides on your back and blocks shots coming from behind you; held in the hands it blocks shots from the front. The angles and what it does not cover are on [Small Items](Small-Items#shield).

## Losing it

A holstered weapon drops with everything else when you die or use `/dropall`.
