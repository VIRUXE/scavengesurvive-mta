# Fuel

![A fuel outlet: Petrol Cans at the pump and a Bobcat waiting](images/scenes/fuel.png)

![Petrol Can](images/items/1650_petrolcanm.png)

Every motor vehicle has a fuel tank. With the engine running it burns fuel all the time, and more the faster you go; when the tank is empty the engine stops and will not start again. Boats and aircraft burn fuel too. The size of each tank is in the table on [Vehicles](Vehicles).

Nearly every vehicle you find has an almost empty tank, so you need petrol: a **Petrol Can** filled at a **fuel outlet**, then poured into the vehicle.

## Petrol Can

A Petrol Can holds up to 20 litres. Cans are found as loot, most often in industrial areas and vehicle trunks, and in [supply drops](Supply-Drops). It is a normal container item (see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)): the amount is shown with the name, for example *Petrol 13.2/20.00*. A can with petrol in it is also used for fuelling [machines](Machines), the Generator and the [BBQ](Campfire-and-BBQ), and to fill an Empty Molotov ([Combining](Combining)).

## Fuel outlets

The pumps at the petrol stations around the map are the fuel outlets. Each has its own stock of fuel that runs down as people fill cans; it starts with some random amount, and a pump that is empty tells you so. Stocks are not refilled while the server runs.

![A fuel station's pumps in San Fierro](images/places/fuel-station.png)

To fill a can: stand within 2 m of a pump, hold the can in your hands and use the interact key (`F`). A progress bar shows the can filling until it is full (20 litres) or the pump is dry.

## Refuelling a vehicle

1. Hold a Petrol Can with fuel in it.
2. Stand next to the vehicle (within about 5 m) and press `F`. Stand at the front or a side: at the rear `F` opens the trunk instead.
3. A 3 second bar runs and up to 10 litres move from the can into the tank.

If the tank is already full you are told so; an empty can is refused. A full tank can be reached with several cans, since one hold moves at most 10 litres.

## Tips

- Small vehicles and bikes use little fuel; trucks and aircraft have big tanks but burn a lot. Check the table on [Vehicles](Vehicles) before you pick one.
- Carry a spare can in your bag or trunk.
- An empty tank is not the only reason an engine refuses to start: it also needs a charged battery, see [Vehicles](Vehicles).
- Lock a fuelled vehicle you leave behind, see [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks).
