# Commands

Type commands in the chat box (`T`, then `/`). Staff commands are not listed here.

## Account

See [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).

| Command | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| `/changepass <old> <new>` | Changes your account password (4 to 32 characters). |
| `/lang <code>` | Sets your language: `en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`, `ptbr`, `ru`, `ro`, `id`, `bhs` or `cs`. Saved on your account. |
| `/tooltips` | Turns the help tips on or off (see [HUD](HUD)). Saved on your account. |
| `/welcome` | Shows the welcome window again. |

## Character

| Command | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| `/craft` | Crafts with what you hold and what is nearby (workbench or ground), or deconstructs with a crowbar in hand. See [Workbench](Workbench) and [Combining](Combining). |
| `/recipes` | Opens the list of crafting recipes. |
| `/dropall` | Drops everything you carry, wear and store on you around your feet, with your hands up, like a [death](Death) without dying. |
| `/aimshout [text]` | Sets the line your [aimshout](Aimshout) shouts; without text it shows how to use it. |
| `/mstats` | Shows your travel stats: distance and time per activity, jumps and time knocked out. The counters last for the session; see [Skills](Skills). |
| `/lock` | Locks or unlocks the vehicle you are in, from any seat. See [Vehicles](Vehicles). |

## Chat and radio

See [Chat](Chat) and [Radio](Radio).

| Command | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| `/g [text]`, `/global [text]` | Switches to global chat, or sends one global message. |
| `/l [text]`, `/local [text]` | Switches to local chat (heard within 40 m), or sends one local message. |
| `/r [text]` | Switches to radio chat, or sends one message to everyone on your frequency. |
| `/me <text>` | Sends an action line. |
| `/radio` | Opens the [radio](Radio) panel. |
| `/freq <87.5-108.0>` | Tunes your radio to a frequency. |
| `/quiet` | Turns your radio's global receiver off or on, hiding global chat. |
| `/chatinfo` | Explains the chat modes. |

## Information

| Command | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| `/help` | Opens the help window. |
| `/rules` | Shows the server rules. |
| `/admins` | Lists the staff and who is online. |
| `/motd` | Shows the message of the day. |
| `/credits` | Shows the credits. |
| `/pos` | Prints your coordinates. |

## Reporting

See [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans) and [Fair Play](Fair-Play).

| Command | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| `/report [name\|near\|killer] [reason]` | Reports a player to the staff. With no arguments a menu opens; `near` picks the closest player within 100 m, `killer` whoever last killed you. |
| `/bug [description]` | Sends a bug report to the staff; without text a window asks for it. |
| `/issues` | Lists the bug reports and opens one. |
