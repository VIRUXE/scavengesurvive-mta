# Trailers & Towing

![The towable Airstream hitched behind a Bobcat](images/scenes/airstream.png)

A few vehicles have a hitch, and three trailers can be hooked onto one: the small utility **Trailer**, the **Articulated** trailer and the **Airstream**, a caravan you can walk into. The trailers standing around the countryside that can be entered but not moved are on [Houses & Trailers](Houses-and-Trailers).

## Hitching

The hitch comes in two sizes and only matches a trailer of its own class: cars and pickups with the small hitch (the Bobcat, Patriot and Yosemite among them) tow the Trailer and the Airstream, the Linerunner's large hitch tows the Articulated trailer.

1. Back the towing vehicle up to the trailer. A trailer is in reach while the two vehicles' centres are closer than both their lengths plus a metre.
2. Press the vehicle secondary fire key, `LCtrl` or `Num 0` on the default bindings (see [Controls](Controls)). The key list shows `Hitch trailer` while a free trailer is in reach and `Unhitch trailer` while you tow one; pressing it again drops the trailer.

Only the driver can hitch. A hitch survives a server restart: the trailer is hooked back onto its truck when the world loads. A trailer torn off in a crash stays off until you hitch it again, but one that comes loose under an empty truck is hooked back on by itself.

The Trailer and the Articulated trailer have trunks of 50 and 100 slots; see the table on [Vehicles](Vehicles). The Airstream has no trunk, no engine, no fuel and cannot be driven by itself.

## The Airstream

The towable Airstream is a vehicle: it is found at vehicle spawn points (rarely), can be locked, and is removed like any vehicle when nobody has touched it for 3 days; opening its door or standing in it counts as use.

**Door.** Stand at the door on foot and press `F` to open or shut it; the key list reads `Open door`, `Close door` or `Door locked`. The door is solid while shut.

**Lock.** The lock sits at the door, not on a driver's side. Keys, a Locksmith Kit, a Lock and Chain, a Crowbar and the Electronic Lockpick all work within reach of the door, inside or outside, and locking shuts the door. A locked door always opens from inside, so nobody is trapped in the room. A [Keypad](Keypads) or Advanced Keypad can be fitted to the door and then takes a four digit code. See [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks).

**Cargo.** Items lying on the Airstream's floor ride along while it is hitched: drop a crate, a bag or a body inside and it travels with you. Their positions are stored when the trailer stops somewhere new and once a minute on the road, so they are still inside after a restart. When the trailer is dropped or torn off, the cargo stays where the trailer stands. A Plot Pole does not ride: its claim stays where it was planted. If the Airstream blows up, everything inside goes with it.

**Gas Bottles.** Two propane mounts sit on the A-frame at the front: a Gas Bottle in hand goes onto a free mount with `F`, empty hands take one off a filled mount (2.5 seconds either way). A trailer found in the world starts with none, one or both. Nothing inside burns that gas yet.

## Armour

The Airstream is the one vehicle that takes **welded armour**: metal [defences](Defences) attached to its shell and welded on with a Welder, which fortifies the door and makes the trailer damage proof while a welded panel remains. Every panel adds 180 kg and costs the towing vehicle 8 % of its engine acceleration, never below 40 %, so an armoured caravan is a slow tow. The process, the gas, the Generator and grinding panels off again are on [Vehicle Armour](Vehicle-Armour).
