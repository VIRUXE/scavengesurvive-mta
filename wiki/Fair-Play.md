# Fair Play

The server watches a few things on its own and acts without waiting for the staff. This page says what gets you kicked or banned and why, so that an honest player is never surprised. Player-made reports are on [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans).

## Kicked

| What | The rule |
|:--|:--|
| **High ping** | Every second your ping is compared with the limit: 400 ms, or 500 ms while 10 or fewer players are online. Ten seconds in a row above it and you are kicked (*ping exceeded ... for 10 seconds*). The first 10 seconds after joining are not counted. |
| **Tabbing out** | Minimising the game or leaving its window unfocused for more than 60 seconds kicks you (*game unfocused for over 60 seconds*). Your character stays in the world while you are away, so do not leave it alone anyway. The staff can turn this off or change the time. |
| **Moving a parked vehicle from outside** | An empty vehicle that jumps 15 m or more while you are the nearest player within 10 m of it gets you kicked (vehicle teleport hacks). |
| **Flooding the server with events** | A client sending far more requests than the game does, or requests the game never makes (modified clients), is kicked. |
| **Chat flooding** | Three lines within one second is a 30 second mute, not a kick; see [Chat](Chat). |
| **Banned alias** | If another name that has played from your address or computer is banned, the staff are told when you log in; with no staff online you are kicked instead (*One of your previously used accounts is banned.*). |

## Banned

These are the automatic bans. They are permanent and recorded against your name, address and computer, so see [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans) if you believe one is a mistake.

- **Jetpack**: there are none on this server; wearing one is a hack.
- **Swimming where there is no water**: the swimming animation in mid-air or on land (fly hacks).
- **An impossible vehicle**: driving a vehicle with more health than any repair on this server can give. Pay 'n' Spray garages are shut for the same reason; repairs are done by hand, see [Repairing & Locks](Repairing-and-Locks).

## Put back and reported

Some things are undone on the spot and filed as a report for the staff rather than punished at once:

- an unexpected position change on foot (you are moved back to where you were);
- moving while frozen by a staff member.

The staff are also warned when an unusual share of your shots at moving players hit, which on its own punishes nobody.

## Other rules the server enforces

- Quitting during a fight counts as dying: [Combat Log](Combat-Log).
- A locked vehicle cannot be entered, and shots are only accepted from the weapon and ammunition you really hold.
- Staff on duty are out of the game: they take no damage from players and are exempt from these checks.
