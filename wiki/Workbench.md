# Workbench

![The Workbench with a Wrench, Hammer, Screwdriver, Welder, Scrap Metal and Refined Metal](images/scenes/workbench.png)

![Workbench](images/items/936_cj_df_worktop_2.png)

A workbench is a work table with a 16-slot container. It is where the complex items are made: electronics, motors, bomb parts, locksmith gear and metal panels. Simple recipes are done by [Combining](Combining), and big built items by [Constructing](Constructing).

## Using a workbench

1. Put the ingredients inside the workbench (it opens like a box, see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags)). It must hold exactly the ingredients of one recipe and nothing else; any stray item means no recipe matches.
2. Equip the tool the recipe asks for ([Screwdriver](Tools) or [Hammer](Tools)).
3. Stand at the workbench and press the interact key (`F`), or type `/craft`. If the contents match no recipe, the workbench simply opens.
4. You turn to the bench and work for 3.6 seconds per ingredient (shortened by [skill](Skills)). Taking an item out of the bench while you work cancels the craft.
5. The ingredients are used up and the result lies on top of the workbench.

<!-- shot:shot-crafting-window -->

Making things trains the skill named after the result.

## Finding and building one

Eleven workbenches stand at fixed places, most of them in the countryside; if one is taken down or carried off it is put back when the server restarts. The list and the map are on [World & Zones](World-and-Zones#fixed-items). You can build your own with a Hammer from 2 Refined Metal and 4 Wood Logs, see [Constructing](Constructing) (the logs come from [trees](Trees-and-Wood)). Hold a Crowbar to take one down.
