## Wearing and removing

Putting a mask on works like any gear ([Clothes & Hats](Clothes-and-Hats)):

- hold it and press `F`, or
- choose `Wear` on it in the inventory list, or
- click the empty `FACE` tile while holding it.

The tile then shows a turning 3D model of the mask with its name under it. Click the tile to take it off: it goes into your hands, or into your inventory if your hands are full. Putting a mask on while you already wear one swaps the old one out the same way. A mask stays on through a server restart and goes to the ground with everything else when you die ([Death](Death)).

## On every skin

A mask sits on the face of every one of the 310 human skins in the game, men and women, large and small, and so does a hat or a vest. Skins that were never fitted individually borrow the fit of a similar build, and a few noses that poked through a bandana have been nudged clear. The character's own skin never changes: the mask is a model attached to the head bone that everyone around you sees.

## What a mask does

Nothing but change your look. A mask does not hide your name in chat, does not change the damage you take, and the Gas Mask does not filter tear gas. A hat is the one piece of headgear with an effect (it cuts the chance of a headshot knocking you out, see [Clothes & Hats](Clothes-and-Hats)); a mask has none.

## Knocked off

A headshot knocks your mask off, hat too if you wear one. Both fall to the ground at your feet and can be picked up again, by you or by whoever shot you.

## Scrap

Only the Gas Mask is worth anything in a Scrap Machine (1 Scrap Metal); the other masks and the bandanas are destroyed by it. See [Scrap](Scrap).
