# Scrap

Scrap Metal is the raw material for building. Many items that have no other use can be broken down into it in a [Scrap Machine](Machines), and a Refining Machine turns Scrap Metal into Refined Metal, the ingredient of workbenches, lockers, large frames and most other builds. See [Machines](Machines) and [Constructing](Constructing).

![Scrap Metal](images/items/19941_goldbar1.png) ![Scrap Machine](images/items/920_y_generator.png)

## How the Scrap Machine works

1. Put items in the machine's 12-slot container (tap `F` at it).
2. Fuel the machine: hold a Petrol Can and hold `F` at the machine (the tank takes up to 100).
3. Hold `F` with empty hands to open the menu, then press Start.
4. The machine cooks for 90 seconds per item plus up to 30 seconds, and uses 3.5 fuel per item. It needs enough fuel for every item inside.
5. When it finishes, the items are replaced by Scrap Metal: one piece per point of scrap value in the table below. A single run gives at most 11 pieces, however much the load is worth. Items that are not in the table are worth nothing and are destroyed.

## Refining

A Refining Machine takes only Scrap Metal. Each Scrap Metal becomes exactly one Refined Metal (12 in, 12 out), with the same cook time and fuel use as the Scrap Machine. It is built from a Gas Cylinder, Fluctuator Unit, Metal Panel, Motor and Power Supply, see [Constructing](Constructing).
