# Joining the Server

Scavenge & Survive Reborn runs on Multi Theft Auto (MTA:SA), a free multiplayer client for the PC version of Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas. You need both the game and the client before you can connect.

## What you need

- **Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas**, the original PC version (1.0). The Definitive Edition does not work with MTA.
- **MTA:SA 1.6**, the client from <https://multitheftauto.com>. The server needs client 1.6.0 or newer.
- An internet connection. There is nothing to download by hand: MTA fetches the server's custom models and scripts on its own when you connect.

You do not need an MTA account, a forum account or a Discord account. Your in-game account is created the first time you connect; see below.

## Install

1. Install GTA: San Andreas and start it once on its own, so that it is set up.
2. Download the MTA:SA 1.6 installer from multitheftauto.com and run it. It finds your GTA installation or asks you where it is.
3. Start MTA:SA. Before you connect, open its settings and set your **nickname**: that name is your account on this server.

## Connect

The server's address is `185.103.74.67:22003`.

In MTA's main menu choose **Quick Connect**, type the address and connect. MTA fetches the server's files, which takes a moment the first time, and drops you into the game.

A friend who already plays can also pull you in through Discord: with Rich Presence on, their status carries a **Join server** button that starts MTA and connects it for you. See [Discord](Discord).

## Your first minutes

1. A window asks for your **language**: pick one of the ten and press **Continue**. See [Languages](Languages).
2. *Register For A New Account* asks for a password of 4 to 32 characters. Your nickname *is* your account: every later connection with that name asks for its password, and a different nickname is a different account. See [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).
3. The welcome text appears, then your character wakes up on a beach with next to nothing. From here, [Getting Started](Getting-Started) walks you through your first hour.

## If it does not work

- *Not whitelisted*: the staff have switched the whitelist on and your name is not on it; see [Account & Login](Account-and-Login).
- *Account already online*: only one session per account; a second client connecting with your name is refused.
- A ban message with its reason and date: see [Reports & Bans](Reports-and-Bans) for appeals.
- Kicked for ping: the server removes players whose ping stays above 400 ms for 10 seconds, and players who stay tabbed out for more than 60 seconds; see [Fair Play](Fair-Play).

See also [Getting Started](Getting-Started), [Controls](Controls) and [FAQ](FAQ).
