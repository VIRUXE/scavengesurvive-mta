# Goggles

Two pairs of goggles let you see what the naked eye cannot: [Night Vision](Night-Vision) and [Thermal Vision](Thermal-Vision). Both are size 2, both are rare military finds, and both run on a [Tool Battery](Tool-Battery).

| Goggles | What they show | A full battery lasts |
|:--|:--|--:|
| [Night Vision](Night-Vision) | The dark world in bright green: buildings, vehicles, items and people stand out on the blackest night. | 40 minutes |
| [Thermal Vision](Thermal-Vision) | Body heat: the living glow orange and yellow against a cold, dark world, by day and by night. | 20 minutes |

## Wearing them

Goggles have a gear tile of their own, `EYES`, next to `HEAD` and `FACE` in the [inventory](Inventory), so they go on together with a hat or helmet and a mask:

- hold them and press `F`, or
- choose `Wear` on them in the inventory list, or
- click the empty `EYES` tile while holding them.

Click the tile to take them off. They stay on through a server restart and fall with everything else when you die ([Death](Death)). A headshot knocks them off, along with your hat and mask.

## Switching them on

Press `J` to switch worn goggles on and off (the `goggles` command, which you can bind to another key in MTA's settings). Your character pulls them down over the eyes; switched off they sit pushed up on the forehead, and everyone around you sees which it is. With a [Flashlight](Flashlight) in your hand the key is shared: a tap of `J` switches the Flashlight, holding it switches the goggles.

- **Night Vision** amplifies what little light there is. It turns the night into day, in green, seen through two tubes that cut off the edges of your view. In daylight the picture burns out, so it is a tool for the dark.
- **Thermal Vision** ignores light and shows warmth. People glow against cold surroundings whatever the hour, in the open or in a dark room, but the world itself is a dim shape: you find people with it, not your way. A body cools once it is dead.

Neither sees through walls.

## Battery

Goggles hold the charge of one [Tool Battery](Tool-Battery). The inventory shows how much is left (`Night Vision (battery 63%)`), and so does the picture itself while they are on. Goggles you find are partly used.

- The battery only runs down while the goggles are switched on.
- Under 15 % the picture starts to flicker; when the battery is flat the goggles switch themselves off.
- To change it, hold a Tool Battery and press `F` while wearing the goggles. The batteries trade places: the fuller one goes in and the old one ends up in your hand with whatever charge it had left, so nothing is wasted.

## Where to find them

Mostly in military loot, now and then in police and survivor loot, and one pair is always in the San Fierro lockup stash. Each item page lists the spawn points.
