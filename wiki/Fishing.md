# Fishing

![Fishing Rod](images/items/18632_fishingrod.png) ![Fish](images/items/19630_fish1.png)

A **Fishing Rod** (size 6) catches **Fish** from any water: the sea, lakes and rivers. Fishing is slow and chancy, but a fish is a big meal and the [Fishing skill](Skills) makes it quicker and surer.

## Casting

1. Hold the Fishing Rod and stand facing the water.
2. Hold `F`. A `Casting` bar fills for as long as you hold the key, up to 10 seconds. Every 0.1 s of hold is 1 m of line, so a 2 s hold casts 20 m and a full 10 s hold casts 100 m.
3. Let go. The line lands that far straight ahead of you. That spot has to be water, and its surface no more than a metre above where you stand (you can fish down from a pier or a bank). On dry land you are told *You need to be closer to the water*.

While the line is out you see *Fishing... Press [F] to cancel*. You cannot cast from a vehicle, and only one line can be out at a time.

## Waiting for a bite

The line moves after **30 to 120 seconds**. When it does, one of two things happens:

- *Unlucky!* The line comes back empty and you can cast again.
- *The line is tugging...* Your character braces and the fish lands at your feet a moment later: 0.1 s per metre of line, so 2 s after a 20 m cast.

Without skill the bite lands a fish **30 %** of the time. Every bite that lands trains the Fishing skill, which both shortens the wait and raises the chance: at full skill the wait is halved (15 to 60 s) and the chance is **65 %**. See [Skills](Skills).

## Stopping

Pressing `F` again reels the line in. Dropping the rod, dying or leaving the server cancels too, and so does a restart of the server while your line is out.

## The catch

The fish arrives as `Fish (Uncooked)`: 4 bites of 4.5 food, and raw it gives only 70 % of that and a [food infection](Infection). Cook it on a [Campfire or BBQ](Campfire-and-BBQ) first. See [Food & Drink](Food-and-Drink).

A Fish is **size 9**, so it never fits the 6-slot [inventory](Inventory). Carry it in your hands, or put it in a bag of nine slots or more (Backpack, Medium Bag, Large Backpack, Parachute Bag or Travel Rucksack), a vehicle trunk or a box; see [Containers & Bags](Containers-and-Bags).
