# Radio

![Radio](images/items/2966_mobile1993b.png)

Everyone has a built-in radio for talking to other players; you do not need to carry anything. It also drives your chat channel: your radio decides who hears your messages.

## Chat channels

The radio has three channels: local (`/l`, players within 40 m), global (`/g`, everyone) and radio (`/r`, everyone on your frequency). Who reads each, how the lines look, voice and the flood mute are on [Chat](Chat); the commands on [Commands](Commands). Your frequency is saved with your character, and every login starts in global.

## Frequencies

Frequencies run from 87.5 to 108.0 MHz, and the default is 98.12. Two radios hear each other when their frequencies are less than 0.05 apart, so 101.30 and 101.34 hear each other but 101.30 and 101.40 do not. Set a frequency with `/freq 101.3`.

## The radio panel

Open the panel with `/radio`, from the **Radio** row at the bottom of your [inventory](Inventory), or by pressing `R` in a vehicle (with a reloadable firearm in hand, hold `R` for a moment; a tap reloads). Close it with Escape or the Close button.

<!-- shot:shot-radio-panel -->

- The two red **knobs** at the sides tune the frequency down and up by 0.5.
- Click the **frequency** to type one; the mouse wheel over it fine-tunes by 0.01.
- The **global / freq** button switches between the global channel and your frequency.
- The **on / off** button turns your transmitter on or off. Off means local chat. Switching it on again returns to the channel you used last.

Your current frequency is always shown on your [watch](Watch).

The Radio item itself (size 1) has no function; it is only worth scrap.
