# Bobcat salvage parts

Twelve standalone items, model ids 20210–20221: tailgate, left and right doors, hood, front and rear bumpers, each Good/Bad. Geometry is the original Bobcat ok/dam component, including broken glass on damaged doors. UVs and dimensions are preserved. Paint uses the stock Bobcat pairing 96/25 (grey and charcoal), with vehicle paint markers converted to fixed material colours. Original texture names, texture alpha and material alpha are retained; vehicle-only reflection/specular plugins are removed for standalone objects. Preview shaders multiply texture colours by material tints and preserve glass/decal alpha; origins are centred at ground level. Each has a box collision, combined Bobcat/vehicle TXD and a two-handed carry configuration. All twelve variants spawn at industrial world loot points (`world_industrial`), with weight 0.6 for good parts and 1.2 for bad parts. They do not spawn inside vehicle containers.

## Mechanics

Slots per model live in `vehicles/shared/logic/bodyparts.lua` (`MODELS`): hood, tailgate, left and right door, front and rear bumper for the Bobcat (422), each with its MTA door or panel id and its point in the vehicle's frame. A part item is `<prefix><Slot>Good|Bad`. Another model joins with a `MODELS` entry plus its twelve part items and models.

- **Remove:** hold a **Wrench**, stand within 1.8 m of the part, **hold F for 0.5 s** (a tap is still the Wrench's engine repair at the bonnet). The vehicle must be unlocked, parked and engine-off. A 4 s hold (Repair skill time modifier) follows; moving over 0.8 m, changing the held item, entering a vehicle or death cancels it, and one action per vehicle is allowed. The slot goes to missing (door 4, panel 3) and the part lands on the ground beside the slot: a Good item if the slot was intact or dented (state 0-1), a Bad item if it hung broken (2-3). `vehicle_part_remove` checks the Wrench is still held, writes the damage and creates the item in one transaction.
- **Install:** hold a part, stand by its slot, press **F**. The slot must be **empty**: replacing a part means removing it first. A 4 s hold, same cancel rules. A Good part leaves the slot at state 0, a Bad part at state 2. `vehicle_part_install` consumes the held item and writes the damage together.
- **Crash drops:** once a second `bodySrv.scan` compares each slot with the last look; a slot that went missing since drops a Bad item at its point, written with the damage by `vehicle_parts_detached`. Slots already missing at spawn, or taken off or put on by a player, never drop.
- Damage (`vehicles.damage`: panels, side doors, lights, tyres, plus `hood` and `tailgate`) is flushed every 60 s by `vehicle_flush`, and written at once by every part change, tyre fix and headlight fix.
- Parts spawn at industrial world loot points (`world_industrial`, weight 0.6 good / 1.2 bad), not inside vehicle containers.
