## Rework 2026-10-23 (evening): crates as upstream has them

The first live run of step 23 (`docs/reviews/2026-10-23-m8-live.md`) showed the deviation was wrong in play: a
small `LargeBox` item for the supply drop, a `GunCase` frame for the cache and the overflow strewn around them.
Rebuilt to match upstream: the two 3799 crate objects stay on the landing spot, the cache is object 964, each is a
32-unit `supply` container with nothing on the ground, persisted in `world_crates` (DDL in the commit body).
`drops.splitForCrate` / `ringPositions` are gone; `drops.crateObjects`, `drops.crateAt`, `drops.TITLES` and
`drops.CRATE_SIZE` replace them. `items:openSupplyContainer` shows a container that has no world item behind it
(no "Pick up container" row). Found on the way: `db.exec` callbacks receive `(ok, ctx, affected, insertId)`, and
the safebox-first-open path in `items/server/containers.lua` indexed the boolean (fixed in passing).
