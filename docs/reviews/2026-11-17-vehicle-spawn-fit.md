# Vehicle spawn fit check (2026-11-17)

`python -m tools.spawn_ground` (sa-geom) lays every vehicle type a point can roll on the ground there, both headings,
and refuses a heading when the body runs into collision (`blocked`: a wall, fence, pole, trunk, roof reaching more
than 15 cm into the vehicle's box, so mirrors may graze), rests propped on something higher than the ground under it
(`propped`, > 0.4 m) or stands steeper than 35 degrees (`steep`). A type that fits one way always faces that way; one
that fits neither way is in `vehicle_spawn_removed` and the point never rolls it. Live since 2026-11-17.
